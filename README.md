# Endurance

*A method for building your own personal AI assistant — one that remembers, travels with you, and gets more useful the more you feed it.*

Endurance turns a general AI (Claude, ChatGPT, or monday.com) into a personal assistant with a real memory. You keep a simple folder of plain-text notes; your assistant reads and writes them, journals what you do, and travels to your phone. No coding required — each setup is a guided, conversational walkthrough.

## Start here

Pick the platform you use and open its setup wizard. Upload it to your assistant and say *"Walk me through setting up my personal assistant."*

- **[Claude](claude-wizard.md)** — best in the desktop app, where it can build everything for you automatically.
- **[ChatGPT](chatgpt-wizard.md)** — Projects + Custom GPTs, with voice on mobile.
- **[monday.com](monday-wizard.md)** — Sidekick + boards, for a structured-data approach.

Not sure which? The **[Master Guide](master-guide.md)** compares all three and helps you choose.

## What you'll end up with

- A tidy, plain-text "memory" that you own.
- An assistant you greet by name to wake up.
- Built-in good habits: short answers by default, a check before saving sensitive info, a journal that doubles as memory, and a plan for when chats get long.
- Your assistant on your phone — plus an upgrade ladder for when you want more.
